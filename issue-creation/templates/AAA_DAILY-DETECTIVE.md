## Why are we doing this?

As a team, we complete daily/weekly checks of our pipelines to ensure they are compliant and there are no critical vulnerabilities.

Whilst the check itself is a Operational Support, if the check uncovers any critical issues - we want to create transparency / prioritisation of this work as it is in addition to our sprint goals so they are logged under this task and must be completed ASAP (Priority 1).
